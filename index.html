Commercial EPC

Energy performance certification for non-domestic buildings, sales and lettings.

Tap for details

Commercial EPC

  • Rates a commercial building’s energy efficiency on an A–G scale.
  • Includes recommendations that can help improve the energy rating.
  • Commercial EPCs are normally valid for 10 years unless replaced by a newer certificate.
  • Required when qualifying commercial property is being marketed for sale or rent.
  • New-build commercial properties require an On Construction certificate.

Fire Alarm Certificate

Inspection and testing of fire alarm systems with formal certification.

Tap for details

Fire Alarm Certificate

  • Testing is carried out with reference to BS 5839.
  • Smoke and heat detectors are checked for correct operation.
  • Sounders, alarm panels, batteries and accessible cable connections are assessed.
  • Grade A systems may include detectors, manual call points, control panels and sounders.
  • Completed testing should be recorded and supported by a fire alarm certificate.
